## Document Overview

This document creates the comprehensive employee form for adding and editing employees. Builds multi-section form with validation following Sri Lankan formats. Implements personal information section with NIC validation, contact information with +94 phone format, employment information with department/position selection, and document upload section for NIC copy, contracts, and certificates.
